How to hire a product

In the hire view you see a scroll panel with all the available items. Just select the quantity of items you want and click "Add"

The items with a spinner in their panel have an unit cost and depend on the number of people you are inviting to your party

Items without the spinner has a fixed group cost by groups of 10 people, the app will automatically make the bill for you

Filtering the products

In the top panel you can see some checkboxes, these are use for filtering the products. Enable the types of products you want to see.
