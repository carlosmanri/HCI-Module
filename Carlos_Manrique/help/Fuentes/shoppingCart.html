The shopping cart view

In the shopping cart you can see how many items do you have in the cart. Here you can select the number of people do you want to invite to yhe party, in order to change it, just click in the spinner on the top of the screen.

Each product has a panel where you can remove it from the cart or change the number of units you want. Just notice the button remove at the left of the panel

You can also delete the whole cart by pressing the button "Remove all"

The price is seen at the top right corner of the screen, it goes updating while you change your cart

Remember that you can login at your account from here with the button "Log in without losing all your products"
