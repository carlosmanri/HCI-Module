How to log in the application

To log in the app, fill the form at the start. If your information is correct, the app will start configuring your party.

You can also use the full app without login and just accesing as a guest using the button "Continue without login"

How to register

If you want to create a new account, click in the "Register button and fill the form, you just need to type your user and password. The information will be remembered for the next time"
